Our Story

Born from a Passion
for the Extraordinary

Noir was conceived in the candlelit corridors of Parisian imagination — a vision of what Lagos deserved but had never seen. A place where the ritual of dining becomes theatre, where every plate tells a story, and every evening dissolves into memory.

Our Executive Chef trained under Michelin-starred kitchens in Lyon and Paris before returning home to craft something uniquely ours — French technique married to West African soul. The result is cuisine that feels both timeless and alive.

Smart elegant attire is required. We welcome refined African wear, cocktail dresses, and formal suits. Jeans, trainers, caps, and casual t-shirts are not permitted. Dressing for the occasion is part of the Noir experience.

Absolutely. Our kitchen takes dietary requirements very seriously. Whether vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, nut allergies, or halal — please indicate your needs in the Special Requests field when reserving, and our Chef will craft your menu accordingly. We ask for at least 24 hours notice for special dietary menus.

Our two private dining rooms seat 8 and 14 guests respectively and can be reserved for proposals, anniversaries, corporate dinners, and private celebrations. Simply note "Private Dining" in your reservation request with your guest count and preferred date. Our events team will contact you within 24 hours to confirm details and menu options.

Noir is located on Adeola Odeku Street, Victoria Island, Lagos. We have dedicated valet parking available every evening from 5:30 PM at no additional charge. Simply pull up to the entrance and our valet team will take care of your vehicle.
